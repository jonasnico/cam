Follow your movement

    
Your face or either hand can play on its own. Stay still to let the sound fade.

    

      
Find a melody
Move your head sideways, or a hand up and down. The brightest tracking marks show which movement is choosing the note.

      
Shape the sound
Move more to add energy. A hand on the right side of the mirror changes brightness; a hand on the left adds gentle detuning.

      
Make some space
Keep your face and hands inside the camera view. Toggle Face or Hands to focus on one way of playing.
